Meet the Owner

A Lifetime Dedicated to Clean Water

Chris Kooi founded ProjectH2O with one clear mission: to bring the same deep water treatment expertise he'd spent over two decades developing at some of the region's most respected water companies directly to the homes and businesses of southwest Michigan — with personal service and genuine accountability that only a locally owned company can offer.

With more than 20 years in the water treatment industry, Chris has seen it all — from stubborn iron and sulfur problems in rural well water to complex commercial filtration challenges in restaurants and healthcare facilities. His hands-on experience as both a frontline service specialist and a senior manager has given him an encyclopedic understanding of water chemistry, treatment equipment, and what it really takes to solve water quality problems the right way.

Over two decades working in all aspects of the water treatment industry — from residential installations and service calls to large commercial projects and management. Chris has personally helped hundreds of Michigan families and businesses improve their water quality.

Why Chris Started ProjectH2O

After spending his career helping build other companies' water treatment businesses, Chris recognized an opportunity to do things differently — to create a company that puts the customer first, every single time, without the bureaucracy and upselling that often comes with large franchises.

"I've spent my entire career in this industry, and I know what it takes to truly solve someone's water problems," says Chris. "ProjectH2O is about bringing that expertise directly to the people of southwest Michigan — with honest assessments, fair pricing, and service you can actually count on."

With deep roots in the community and a reputation built entirely on results and referrals, ProjectH2O is quickly becoming the trusted water treatment company for families and businesses from Kalamazoo to Allegan and throughout the surrounding region.
