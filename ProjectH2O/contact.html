FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free water testing work? ▾
We schedule a convenient time to visit your home or business and perform an on-site water analysis using a professional test kit. We test for hardness, iron, pH, TDS, and other parameters relevant to your situation. You get the results explained clearly, along with our honest recommendations — completely free and with no obligation to purchase.
How long does a water softener installation take? ▾
Most residential water softener installations take between 2–4 hours. We handle everything from connecting the unit to the plumbing to programming the settings for your specific water hardness. We clean up completely before we leave.
Do I need to be home for the service visit? ▾
For initial water testing and system installations, yes — we'll need access to your home or business. For some maintenance calls like salt delivery, we can work with you to find a convenient arrangement.
What makes Clover water coolers different? ▾
Clover is a globally recognized leader in water dispenser technology, known for award-winning design, multi-stage filtration, and exceptional reliability. Their systems deliver hot, cold, and room-temperature purified water on demand. Unlike generic coolers, Clover units are built to last and backed by strong warranty support — and when you buy through ProjectH2O, you get local installation and service too.
Do you serve well water customers? ▾
Absolutely — in fact, well water often has more complex treatment needs than municipal water, and we have extensive experience solving iron, sulfur, hardness, and bacterial issues common in southwest Michigan's private wells. Our free water test is especially valuable for well owners.
What areas do you service? ▾
We serve southwest Michigan, including Kalamazoo, Portage, Battle Creek, Allegan, Otsego, Plainwell, Paw Paw, Three Rivers, and the surrounding communities. If you're not sure if we cover your area, just give us a call at (269) 539-1185 and we'll let you know right away.
