Water Softeners

Clear Water, Healthy Systems

Southwest Michigan has some of the hardest water in the state. Hard water causes scale buildup on pipes and appliances, leaves spots on dishes and fixtures, dries out skin and hair, and reduces the efficiency of your water heater by up to 30%.

A ProjectH2O water softener delivers the clear water your home deserves — custom-sized to your household, engineered for efficiency, and built to last for years with minimal maintenance.

  • Eliminate scale on pipes, appliances, and fixtures
  • Softer skin, healthier hair, and brighter laundry
  • Extend the life of dishwashers, water heaters & more
  • Use less detergent and cleaning products
  • Smart metered softeners for maximum salt efficiency
  • Professional sizing, installation, and service

Filtration Systems

We Kill Bacteria, Not Your Peace of Mind.

Michigan groundwater and municipal supplies can contain iron, hydrogen sulfide, chlorine, nitrates, heavy metals, bacteria, and other contaminants that affect taste, odor, and health. Our filtration systems are designed specifically for the water challenges common in southwest Michigan — eliminating threats without the worry.

  • Iron & manganese filtration — eliminate rust stains
  • Sulfur removal — eliminate rotten egg odors
  • Whole-home carbon filtration for chlorine & taste
  • Reverse osmosis drinking water systems
  • UV purification for bacteria and viruses
  • Under-sink point-of-use filtration
  • Sediment filters for well water

Free Water Testing

Know Your Water Before You Commit

We believe in transparency. Before recommending any system, we perform a complimentary water quality analysis to understand exactly what's in your water. This no-pressure testing helps us design the right solution for your specific situation.

  • Total hardness & calcium levels
  • Iron and manganese concentration
  • pH balance and alkalinity
  • Hydrogen sulfide (odor testing)
  • Total dissolved solids (TDS)
  • Chlorine and chloramine levels
  • Nitrate screening for well water
